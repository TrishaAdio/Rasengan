Summoning + mount/ride - live dedicated-server audit
====================================================

Produced by: tools/verify/run_summon_test.sh
Minecraft 26.1.2 / NeoForge 26.1.2.109, dedicated server, flat world,
pause-when-empty-seconds=0, 225 forceloaded chunks.

Two synthetic ServerPlayers in the real PlayerList over a socket-less Connection, with
outbound packets recorded rather than written. doTick() is called by the harness because
ServerPlayer.tick() does not call Player.tick() in 26.1.

Rider input is fed through ServerPlayer.setLastClientInput, i.e. vanilla's own replicated
Input record - the same field the real ServerboundPlayerInputPacket writes. The harness does
not call the launch directly; it presses W and lets the server's own detector decide.

NOT verified here: anything visual or audible. No display, no audio device, no real client.
See the 'Not verified' sections of MOUNT.md and SUMMONING.md.
